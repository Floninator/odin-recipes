A burger is a popular sandwich consisting of a cooked patty of ground meat, usually beef, placed inside a sliced bun or roll. It is often garnished with various toppings such as lettuce, tomato, cheese, pickles, onions, and condiments like ketchup and mustard. This recipe will guide you through the steps to create a delicious homemade burger that will satisfy your cravings.

Ingredients:

  • 1 hamburger bun
  • 1 patty of ground beef
  • 1 slice of cheese
  • lettuce leaves
  • tomato slices
  • pickles
  • onion slices
  • ketchup
  • mustard

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the grill or a skillet to medium-high heat.
  2. Place the patty on the grill or in the skillet and cook for about 4-5 minutes on each side, or until fully cooked.
  3. While the patty is cooking, toast the hamburger bun halves on the grill or in the skillet.
  4. Layer the ingredients in the following order: bottom bun, lettuce, tomato, pickles, onion, cheese, patty, ketchup, mustard, top bun.
  5. Serve immediately and enjoy!
