Lasagna

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese. It is baked in the oven until bubbly and golden brown. This recipe will guide you through the steps to create a delicious lasagna that will impress your family and friends.

Ingredients:

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup parmesan cheese

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  3. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef over medium heat until browned. Drain any excess fat.
  4. Add the marinara sauce to the cooked beef and simmer for 5 minutes.
  5. In a separate bowl, mix the ricotta cheese with 1/2 cup of parmesan cheese.
  6. In a 9x13 inch baking dish, spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom.
  7. Layer 4 lasagna noodles over the sauce, followed by a layer of ricotta mixture, then a layer of mozzarella cheese. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, ending with a layer of meat sauce and mozzarella cheese on top.
  8. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  9. Let the lasagna cool for 10 minutes before serving. Enjoy!
