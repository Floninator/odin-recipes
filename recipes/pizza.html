Pizza is a beloved dish that originated in Italy and has become popular worldwide. It consists of a round, flat base of dough topped with various ingredients such as tomato sauce, cheese, vegetables, and meats. This recipe will guide you through the steps to create a delicious homemade pizza that will satisfy your cravings.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 cup pepperoni slices
  • 1/2 cup mushrooms, sliced
  • 1/2 cup bell peppers, sliced

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface to your desired thickness.
  3. Transfer the dough to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  4. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a border for the crust.
  5. Shred the mozzarella cheese and sprinkle it over the sauce.
  6. Add the parmesan cheese on top of the mozzarella.
  7. Arrange the pepperoni slices, mushrooms, and bell peppers on top of the cheese.
  8. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly.
  9. Let the pizza cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving. Enjoy!
